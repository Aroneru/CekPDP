# Pixel Pals: Privacy

Pixel Pals is a free game made by Studio Kancil Interaktif.

When you play, the game collects your device ID, IP address and gameplay statistics such as levels completed and session length. We use this information to run the game, save your progress and show ads.

Ads in Pixel Pals are served by advertising partners, who may use your device ID to show ads.

Game data is stored on servers in the United States.

Pixel Pals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal data, contact support@pixelpals.example and we will delete it.

Questions: support@pixelpals.example
