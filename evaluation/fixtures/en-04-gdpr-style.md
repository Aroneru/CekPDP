# Lingowave Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 June 2026

## 1. Controller

Lingowave Learning Ltd, 1 Example Square, Dublin, Ireland, is the controller of your personal data. Questions about this notice, and requests to exercise your rights, can be sent to our Data Protection Officer at dpo@lingowave.example or submitted through the Privacy Centre in the app.

## 2. Data we process, why, and our lawful basis

- Account data (name, email address and password hash) is used to create and run your account. Lawful basis: performance of a contract.
- Learning data (lesson progress and quiz answers) is used to provide lessons and personalised exercises. Lawful basis: performance of a contract.
- Device and usage data (IP address, device model and app events) is used to keep the service secure and to understand how features are used. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
- Marketing preferences (email address and newsletter choices) are used to send newsletters. Lawful basis: consent.

## 3. Where the data comes from

We collect data from you when you register and use the app, automatically through the app's analytics SDK and server logs, and from the app store when you buy a subscription (purchase confirmation only).

## 4. Consent

Where we rely on consent, we ask for it with a separate opt-in that is unticked by default.

## 5. Recipients

We share personal data with cloud hosting and analytics providers acting as our processors, with payment processors, and with public authorities where the law requires it.

## 6. International transfers

Your data is stored in data centres in the European Union and the United States. Transfers outside Indonesia and the European Union are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses, which are binding written agreements.

## 7. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this notice, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Account and learning data are processed while your account is active, and marketing data until you withdraw your consent.

## 8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law you have the right to:

- access your personal data and obtain a copy of it;
- rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
- have your data erased;
- restrict processing;
- data portability, meaning you can receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller;
- object to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal or similarly significant effects;
- withdraw your consent at any time in the app's Privacy Centre; and
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## 9. Response times

We respond to rights requests within one month, which we may extend by two further months for complex requests.

## 10. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls and regular security testing.

## 11. Personal data breaches

If a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will notify you without undue delay. We will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required.

## 12. Corporate transactions

If we sell or reorganise our business, personal data may be transferred to the buyer or successor, subject to this notice.

## 13. Changes to this notice

We will tell you about material changes by email or in-app message before they take effect.
