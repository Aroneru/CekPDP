# Kanopi Workspace Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

## Who we are

Kanopi Workspace is operated by PT Kanopi Kerja Digital, a limited liability company registered in Indonesia with its office at Jl. Contoh Teknologi No. 5, Bandung ("Kanopi", "we"). PT Kanopi Kerja Digital is the personal data controller responsible for the processing described in this policy.

## Personal data we collect

- Account details: your full name, work email address, job title and password (stored as a salted hash).
- Workspace content: the names and email addresses of colleagues you invite, and the comments you post.
- Billing details: company name, billing contact, invoices and payment status. Card numbers are handled by our payment provider and never reach our servers.
- Technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers and usage logs.

## How we obtain it

We collect most data directly from you when you sign up, invite colleagues or contact support. We collect technical data automatically through cookies and server logs. If your employer sets up your account, we receive your name and work email address from your employer.

## Why we use your data and on what basis

We use your personal data to:

- provide and secure the service, under our contract with you or your employer;
- process payments and keep accounting records, to meet our legal obligations;
- prevent fraud and abuse, based on our legitimate interests; and
- send product news and webinar invitations, only with your explicit consent.

## Consent

Where we rely on consent, we ask for it separately with an unticked checkbox, and we record when and how you gave it. Using the service does not, by itself, mean you consent to marketing.

## Who we share it with

We share personal data with:

- hosting and email delivery providers that process data on our behalf under written contracts;
- our payment provider, to collect subscription fees; and
- government authorities where Indonesian law requires us to disclose it.

We do not sell personal data.

## International transfers

Our servers are located in Singapore, so your personal data is transferred outside Indonesia. We transfer data only where the recipient country offers protection equivalent to Indonesian law or, where it does not, under binding written agreements that require the recipient to protect your data to that standard.

## How long we keep it

We keep account data for as long as your subscription is active and delete it 90 days after the subscription ends. Invoices are kept for 10 years to meet tax obligations. Usage logs are kept for 12 months.

## Your rights

You can:

- access your personal data and receive a copy of it;
- correct or complete inaccurate data;
- ask us to stop processing your data and to delete or destroy it;
- withdraw your consent at any time, through Settings > Notifications or by emailing us;
- ask us to delay or restrict processing; and
- receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format (CSV or JSON) and ask us to transmit it to another provider.

## How to make a request

Email privacy@kanopi.example or use the Privacy Request form in your account settings. We log each request and confirm receipt. We respond to requests within 14 days.

## Security

We encrypt data in transit with TLS and at rest with AES-256, restrict staff access on a need-to-know basis with single sign-on and two-factor authentication, and run annual penetration tests.

## Security incidents

If a security incident affects your personal data, we will notify you without undue delay and explain what happened and what we are doing about it.

## Data protection officer

Our data protection officer can be reached at dpo@kanopi.example.

## Business transfers

If Kanopi is involved in a merger or acquisition, your personal data may be transferred to the new owner.

## Changes to this policy

We will email account owners at least 30 days before any change to this policy takes effect.
